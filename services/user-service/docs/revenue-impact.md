# User Service - Revenue Impact

## COD Reputation Scoring = Revenue Protection
- High reputation users: 90% COD completion rate
- Low reputation users: 50% COD completion rate
- Revenue protection: 15-20% reduction in fraud-driven leakage

## User Verification = Trust
- Verified sellers: 2x conversion rate vs. unverified
- KYC completion rate: 60% target

## Multi-Role Users = Network Effects
- Users who are both buyers and sellers: 3x LTV
- Cross-role conversion: 15% of buyers become sellers
