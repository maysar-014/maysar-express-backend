# Support Service - Revenue Impact

## Fast Resolution = Reduced Churn
- Resolution time <24h: 90% satisfaction
- Resolution time >48h: 50% satisfaction
- Each 1% churn reduction: TZS 1.8B/year revenue protection

## Self-Service = Cost Efficiency
- Self-service deflection: 40% of tickets
- Cost per ticket: TZS 5,000 (agent time)
- Savings: TZS 100M/year at scale

## Satisfaction = Retention
- High satisfaction (>4/5): 85% retention
- Low satisfaction (<3/5): 40% retention
- Support quality: #2 factor in retention (after product)
