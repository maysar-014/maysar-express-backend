# Commission Service - Revenue Impact

## Commission Model = Revenue Engine
- Platform take rate: 8-12% of GMV
- Seller base: 8% of GMV
- Enabler share: 15-20% of task value
- Quality bonus: Up to +18% of base (incentivizes excellence)

## Quality Bonus = Seller Retention
- Sellers with quality bonus: 80% retention rate
- Sellers without: 50% retention rate
- Revenue impact: 30% higher LTV for quality sellers

## Transparent Calculation = Trust
- Real-time commission visibility
- Dispute rate: <1% (industry: 5%)
- Seller satisfaction: 85% (commission fairness)
