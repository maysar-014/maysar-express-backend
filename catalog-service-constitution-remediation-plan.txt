# CATALOG SERVICE ARCHITECTURE CONSTITUTION REMEDIATION PLAN

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Catalog Service currently violates multiple Architecture Constitution principles due to incomplete implementation.
This remediation plan outlines the systematic approach to achieve full constitutional compliance.

## CONSTITUTION VIOLATION ASSESSMENT

### CRITICAL VIOLATIONS (Must Fix - Priority 1)
1. **Business Core Protection Principle (Section 3)** - No domain business logic implemented
2. **Domain Law (Section 4)** - Domain layer incomplete, purity cannot be verified
3. **Saga Orchestration Law (Section 6)** - No saga orchestrators exist
4. **Infrastructure Isolation Rule (Section 8)** - No infrastructure adapters
5. **DTO Firewall Rule (Section 9)** - No API layer for boundary control

### MAJOR VIOLATIONS (Should Fix - Priority 2)
6. **File Structure Mandate (Section 10)** - Structure exists, content missing
7. **Testing Constitution (Section 11)** - No test suites implemented
8. **Engines/Policies/Algorithms Separation (Section 5)** - Not implemented
